Method & environment

Procedure nominal.benchmark(locale, seed, count, null) loads the locale dictionaries once, calls the complete contact procedure for each position, serializes all fields, and folds them into a running MD5 checksum. The loop cannot skip data generation. SQL wall time includes generation, serialization, and hashing; it excludes network transport and HTML rendering. Client time includes the complete round trip.

Aggregate throughput is total contacts divided by total SQL seconds, rather than an average of per-run rates. Benchmarks run sequentially against a shared VPS; these are measurements, not a performance guarantee.

Why not just count a million rows?

Counting a series measures the series generator. Here, a measured contact includes name variation, a Markov street name, a formatted address, phone and email, uniform sphere coordinates, normal physical attributes, and eye color.
